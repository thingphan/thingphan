## Selected Projects

### [CMAgent](https://github.com/thingphan/CMAgent-multi-agent)
**Multi-agent curation of cell type–marker relationships**

- Coordinate extraction, verification, and normalization agents to turn biomedical evidence sentences into structured relationships.
- Map cell and gene mentions to Cell Ontology and NCBI Gene identifiers.
- Preserve supporting evidence and provenance in CSV and relational databases.

### [Seoul Eco Routing](https://github.com/thingphan/seoul-eco-routing)
**Energy-efficient routing for electric vehicles**

- Combine Seoul public data, XGBoost traffic-speed prediction, and a physics-based EV energy model.
- Compare fastest, minimum-energy, and time-constrained routes across 63,548 road links.
- Build a FastAPI dashboard for interactive route comparison.

### [Seoul Community Bus Carbon Analysis](https://github.com/thingphan/seoul-maeul-bus-carbon)
**Demand prediction and fleet allocation for sustainable, accessible transit**

- Analyze carbon efficiency and older adults' transit accessibility across 230 Seoul community bus routes.
- Combine XGBoost quantile regression with mixed-integer linear programming to identify feasible vehicle replacements.
- Submitted to a transportation data analysis competition co-organized by Hankyoreh.
